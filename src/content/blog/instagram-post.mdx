Siege of Limerick is an independent heavy metal gathering held at Dolan’s in Limerick City. Built around accessibility, discovery, and community, the festival brings Irish and international bands together across multiple stages for a full day of loud, uncompromising music.

The event began in 2009 and has grown into a landmark for Ireland’s underground metal scene while keeping its DIY spirit. Most editions are free to attend, making the Siege as much about finding your next favourite band as it is about seeing the established names. It is a proper celebration of the people who keep heavy music moving: bands, fans, volunteers, promoters, and the venue itself.
